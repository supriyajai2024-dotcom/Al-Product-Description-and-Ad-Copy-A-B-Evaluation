# Before-and-after responsible-AI editing

These examples are editorial demonstrations, not claims that unsafe copy appeared in the delivered set.

## Unsupported environmental claim
**Original (illustrative risky wording):** “The planet-saving bottle that cuts plastic waste by 80%.”
**Problem:** Unsupported environmental benefit and fabricated statistic; not among verified facts.
**Revised:** “A reusable water bottle for everyday use, with clear water-level markings.”
**Why:** Uses only established project facts and removes quantitative implication.

## Exaggerated promise
**Original (illustrative risky wording):** “Never forget to drink water again.”
**Problem:** Absolute behavioral promise and implies an unsupported reminder feature.
**Revised:** “Keep a reusable bottle close during your everyday routine.”
**Why:** Describes a practical use context without promising outcomes or features.

## Readability
**Before:** “Whether attending lectures or proceeding to professional responsibilities, hydration management can be simplified.”
**After:** “From class to work, keep your bottle close.”
**Why:** Shorter, concrete wording that is easier to scan.
