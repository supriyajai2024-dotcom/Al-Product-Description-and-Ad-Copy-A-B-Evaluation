# Survey questions and methodology

## Participant-facing introduction
This survey evaluates short advertisements for EcoSip Smart Bottle, a fictional brand and product created for a capstone project. No real product is being sold. Participation is voluntary. Please do not include identifying or sensitive information. Responses will be used for an academic project and summarized in aggregate.

## Questions (ready to copy into Google Forms)
1. Which advertisement do you prefer? (Required; single choice; options: A01–C05 with ad text shown, randomized or balanced order.)
2. How clear is your preferred advertisement? (Required; linear scale 1–5; 1 Not at all clear, 5 Very clear.)
3. How trustworthy does it seem? (Required; 1 Not at all trustworthy, 5 Very trustworthy.)
4. How appealing is it? (Required; 1 Not at all appealing, 5 Very appealing.)
5. How likely would you be to consider this fictional product? (Required; 1 Not at all likely, 5 Very likely.)
6. Why did you select this advertisement? (Optional; paragraph.)
7. Are you aged 18–30? (Optional eligibility check, Yes/No/Prefer not to say. Do not collect name/email.)

## Method
Target: at least 20 genuine volunteers aged 18–30. Present the product facts consistently, hide prompt-style labels during voting, and randomize ad ordering when practical. In Google Forms, collect email addresses OFF, limit-to-one-response OFF unless sign-in is acceptable, and avoid demographic details beyond what is essential. Share the form voluntarily; do not pressure participants. Export responses to CSV and place only genuine responses in the workbook’s Survey Responses sheet. Record consent/ethics rules required by your institution.
